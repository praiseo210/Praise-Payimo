ABOUT PRAISE PAYIMO

Music with purpose.

Praise Payimo is a worship leader and gospel artist whose music is rooted in one simple conviction, that the Word of God is alive, and it deserves to be sung. Drawing inspiration from the miraculous, Praise writes and ministers songs that don't just fill a room but shift the atmosphere in it.

Each song is less a performance and more an invitation, an open door to step out of the noise of everyday life and into something sacred. An invitation to believe that God still moves, still heals, and still shows up for those who seek Him. At the core of his ministry is Worship Culture, an interdenominational worship and miracle meeting where believers from all backgrounds gather to experience God through praise, prayer, and the supernatural. It's a space where worship isn't confined to a denomination. Praise's vision is straightforward but carries weight: to see the truth of Scripture expressed through songs, not just preached from a pulpit, but felt, heard, and experienced.
